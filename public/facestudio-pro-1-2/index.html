Fair questions.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Detection, swapping, editing and export all run on your computer. The only network traffic is setup and the license check.

How many photos can I batch at once?

There is no fixed limit. We test runs of 20 photos, and speed holds steady from the first photo to the last. On a Mac, the app keeps the computer awake until the batch finishes.

Can I keep using the app during a batch?

Yes. Open any other photo and keep editing. If you run a waiting photo by hand, it leaves the batch so the two never collide.

Does batch work on group photos?

Face Swap does: you choose who gets the new face in each group photo. Head Swap works on single-person photos, so groups are skipped with a warning.

Does one purchase cover Mac and Windows?

Yes. A single one-time license covers both versions. Each activation is tied to a machine.
