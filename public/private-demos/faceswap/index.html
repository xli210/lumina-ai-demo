Not perfect yet? You can polish the result yourself in a few clicks:

  1. Zoom in to the face you want to fix.
  2. Turn Brush on and pick a comfortable brush size.
  3. On either image, paint over anything you want pulled from the original — fine details, jewelry, hair edges, products, occluders, or any seam artifacts.
  4. Click Bring back masked region — those areas are instantly restored from the original photo.
  5. Made a mistake while painting? Hit Clear mask. Want the original AI result back? Hit Reset.
  6. Repeat steps 2-4 until you're happy with the result.
