## Schedule

- One short a day. Rotate the five clips across all platforms, then repeat them with different hooks.
- After two weeks, look at GA4 (Traffic acquisition, session source) and keep the platforms that send sign-ups.
- Reply to comments asking "what app is this" with the name and say the link is in the bio. Do not paste links in comments; most platforms limit that.

## Rules (to keep the accounts alive)

- **Label as AI.** Tick the platform's "AI-generated" label: TikTok's AI-generated content toggle, YouTube's "altered or synthetic content" disclosure, Instagram's AI info label. The footer already says so.
- **Use only stock or consented faces.** Never use celebrities, politicians or anyone who has not agreed. The five clips use stock footage and stock faces (Pexels, Mixkit).
- **Nothing sexual or deceptive.** No fake news, no impersonation.
- **Reddit.** Post in communities that allow tool showcases, such as r/aivideo and r/StableDiffusion's showcase threads, and read each sub's self-promotion rules first. One post per sub, written as "here is what it does on hard footage", not an ad.
